ABOUT BEREKA

Healthcare with care at its heart.

Bereka Maternal and Child Health Speciality Center is dedicated to providing compassionate and professional healthcare for mothers, children and families.

Located around Bethel in Addis Ababa, our center focuses on creating a safe, respectful and welcoming healthcare environment for every patient.

We believe that quality healthcare should be available when people need it. Our center therefore provides services 24 hours a day.
