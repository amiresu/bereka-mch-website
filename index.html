ABOUT BEREKA MCH

A center dedicated to the health of mothers and children.

Bereka Maternal and Child Health Speciality Center is committed to providing compassionate and professional healthcare services for mothers, children and families.

Located around Bethel in Addis Ababa, our center is focused on creating a safe, respectful and welcoming environment where patients and families can receive the care they need.

Our approach places the wellbeing of mothers and children at the heart of everything we do.
